Citing eOn¶

Please cite the eOn software through its Zenodo record [CITE_1]. The concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18529495 always resolves to the latest release, and each release also has its own DOI on Zenodo. GitHub reads the same reference from CITATION.cff. The entry below matches docs/source/bibtex/eonDocs.bib, which these pages draw on.

@misc{goswamiTheochemUIEOn2026,
  title = {{{TheochemUI/eOn}}},

Papers on eOn 2.x and later¶

The rewrites behind eOn 2.x and 3.x, including the Gaussian process methods and the socket and calculator interfaces, are covered in Goswami [CITE_2]. For other eOn 2.x and later methods (AKMC, the dimer, Lanczos, and minimization), please also cite Goswami’s 2025 thesis.

If you use one of the methods below, please also cite the paper behind it.

  • Gaussian process regression accelerated dimer (min_mode_method = gprdimer): Goswami et al. [CITE_3].

  • Adaptive pruning and optimal transport for Gaussian process saddle searches (use_prune and related keys under [GPR Dimer]): Goswami and Jónsson [CITE_4].

  • Any nudged elastic band run (job = nudged_elastic_band), including off-path climbing image NEB (OCI-NEB) with Hessian eigenmode alignment: Goswami et al. [CITE_5].

  • Benchmarking saddle searches, and rotation removal in the dimer (remove_rotation): Goswami [CITE_6].

  • Background on Gaussian process acceleration of saddle and minimum searches: Goswami [CITE_7].

  • NEB workflows with eOn and machine-learned potentials: Goswami [CITE_8].

  • Two-dimensional RMSD plots of NEB paths: Goswami [CITE_9].

  • Metatomic potentials in eOn, including the NEB runs: Bigi et al. [CITE_10].

Older papers¶

These describe the original EON code and the methods it started from, oldest first: kinetic Monte Carlo without a lattice [CITE_11], adaptive kinetic Monte Carlo [CITE_12], long time scale simulation of a grain boundary [CITE_13], the distributed EON2 code [CITE_14], and the 2014 software paper [CITE_15].

The pages for each method, such as Adaptive kinetic Monte Carlo, Dimer method and Nudged elastic band, cite the papers those methods come from.

14// Path-integral quantum transition-state theory (Voth, Chandler and
15// Miller, J. Chem. Phys. 91, 7749 (1989)): the centroid potential of mean
16// force along a linear mass-weighted coordinate s from ring polymers whose
17// centroid is held on parallel planes, and the rate from its value on the
18// dividing plane, optionally times the ring-polymer MD transmission factor
19// on that plane (Craig and Manolopoulos, J. Chem. Phys. 122, 084106 (2005);
20// Suleimanov, Allen and Green, Comput. Phys. Commun. 184, 833 (2013)).

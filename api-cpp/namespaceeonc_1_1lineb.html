const std::vector< long > & freeAtoms,

Pack free-atom coordinates into a flat [image][atom][xyz] buffer for the lineb C API.

Adjacent images are joined by the minimum-image step of the reference cell when periodic; otherwise the stored Cartesian values are used. Unwrapped coordinates are never written through Matter::setPositions (which rewraps under PBC). Molecular seeds under PBC are refused as ambiguous without a bond topology; Rust performs mass-weighted alignment.

127// Ring-polymer instanton for the splitting between two minima.
128//
129// A path of P + 1 beads in mass-weighted coordinates q runs from one minimum
130// to the other in imaginary time beta hbar, the ends fixed at the minima. The
131// instanton is the path that minimises the discretised Euclidean action
132//
133// S = sum_j |q_{j+1} - q_j|^2 / (2 dtau) + dtau sum_j' V(q_j),
134//
135// dtau = beta hbar / P, with half weight on the two ends (trapezoid). The
136// splitting follows from the ratio of the off-diagonal to the diagonal
137// imaginary-time propagator, both in the same steepest-descent
138// approximation:
139//
140// delta0 = 2 hbar sqrt(S0 / (2 pi hbar dtau))
141// sqrt(det J_well / det' J) exp(-(S - S_well) / hbar),
142//
143// with J the Hessian of S over the interior beads, det' leaving out its
144// zero mode (the kink's position in imaginary time), S0 the integral of
145// |dq/dtau|^2 and J_well the same Hessian with every bead at a minimum.
146// Time runs in amu^0.5 Angstrom eV^-0.5, so hbar is kHbar. Where the minimum
147// energy path curves, the instanton cuts the corner and follows the
148// transverse zero-point energy, which a one-dimensional WKB integral along
149// the path cannot.

208// Ring-polymer instanton for the thermal rate below the crossover
209// temperature (Richardson and Althorpe, J. Chem. Phys. 131, 214106 (2009)).
210//
211// A closed ring of N beads in mass-weighted coordinates q, beta_N = beta / N,
212// has the potential
213//
214// U_N = sum_j V(q_j) + sum_j |q_{j+1} - q_j|^2 / (2 beta_N^2 hbar^2),
215//
216// q_N = q_0. Below T_c = hbar omega_b / (2 pi kB), omega_b the imaginary
217// frequency at the saddle, the instanton is a first-order saddle of U_N:
218// one negative mode, and one zero mode that cycles the beads. The rate is
219//
220// k Z_r = (1 / (beta_N hbar)) sqrt(B_N / (2 pi beta_N hbar^2))
221// prod'_k 1 / (beta_N hbar |omega_k|) exp(-beta_N U_N),
222//
223// B_N = sum_j |q_{j+1} - q_j|^2, omega_k^2 the eigenvalues of the
224// mass-weighted Hessian of U_N with the zero mode left out, and Z_r the
225// ring-polymer partition function of the harmonic reactant,
226//
227// Z_r = exp(-beta V_r) prod_{k=0}^{N-1} prod_m
228// 1 / (beta_N hbar sqrt(lambda_m + 4 sin^2(pi k / N) / (beta_N
229// hbar)^2)),
230//
231// lambda_m the eigenvalues of the reactant's mass-weighted Hessian. Above
232// T_c the ring collapses onto the saddle and the formula no longer applies.

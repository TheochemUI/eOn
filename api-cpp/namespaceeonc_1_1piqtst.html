k = (1/2) sqrt(2 / (pi beta)) exp(-beta F(s*)) / int_{s_0}^{s*} exp(-beta F(s)) ds, the mass-weighted coordinate carrying unit mass, beta in 1 / eV.

The integral is the trapezoid rule over the planes; the last is s*.

hSaddle and the band in pathQ are in the instanton's mass-weighted coordinates over the free atoms, measured from the reactant; saddle is aligned to the reactant. Appends results.dat keys for the last (lowest) temperature to extras and returns the files written.

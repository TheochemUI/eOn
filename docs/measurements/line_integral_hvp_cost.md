# Line-integral HQTST cost with the shipped ring estimator

This note records the estimator that the tree actually ships. The ring
determinant uses a two-column block-Lanczos start: the imaginary-time cycle
direction and one Gaussian direction. Each stochastic quadrature uses a
quarter of `determinant_estimated_error` as its geometric-tail target, and the
probe standard error stops at half that configured value. The reported sum is
a diagnostic estimate, not a certified bound.

## Benchmark definition

The fresh benchmark uses the CuH2 Cu(110) plus two H regression inputs. Its
ladder has seven energy levels, 10 LI-NEB images, and 150 ring replicas. It ran
on one core and one thread:

```
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 taskset -c 3 eonclient
```

```ini
[Main]
job = line_integral_tunneling

[Potential]
potential = cuh2

[Line Integral Tunneling]
images = 10
replicas = 150
energy_offset = 0.05
energy_step = 0.01
log_rate_tolerance = 1.151292546497023
spring = 20
contour = 1000
force_tolerance = 0.01
period_tolerance = 0.01
lowest_temperature = 230
```

{file}`scripts/line_integral_cuh2_tables.py`. The seven-level rows come from
{file}`docs/source/fig/data/cuh2_line_integral_7level.csv`, generated from the
fresh job's `line_integral_tunneling.dat` and `results.dat`. The longer-run

The five products beyond the sum of the seven per-level rows come from the
Lanczos lowest mode at the saddle, which sets the crossover temperature. The
45-level CSV counts only the P=150 HQTST rate products at each level. Products
used by the separate rate-instanton references and cold image/replica
refinements are not included.

## Analytic products and fallback

For CuH2, every Hessian action in this benchmark is the analytic rgpot
mass-weighted Hessian-vector product, avoiding finite-difference step-size
bias. A potential without an analytic product does not fail merely for that
reason: the job declares a
finite-difference fallback, logs its use and records it in `results.dat` as
`line_integral_finite_difference_hessian_fallback`.

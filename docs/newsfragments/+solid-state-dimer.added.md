Minimization and min-mode saddle search can relax the cell with the atoms. `[Solid State] enabled` is off by default, so a frozen cell is unchanged. The joint coordinate is the Sheppard, Xiao and Henkelman Jacobian J = V^(1/3) N^(1/6), with a mask on the six lower-triangular strains and an external stress. Stiff cell directions are scaled to an atomic curvature on top of that Jacobian. `max_strain_step` limits one strain update and `max_strain` stops a cell that runs away. `min_mode_method` dimer and lanczos estimate the lowest mode in that space. `results.dat` then carries the Cauchy stress and the elastic dipole P = V sigma at the minimum and the saddle. `potential = eam_cu` reads a one-element eam/alloy file, including Mishin copper, and returns the virial.

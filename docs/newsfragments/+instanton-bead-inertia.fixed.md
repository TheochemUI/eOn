The rate-instanton search keeps each bead block's count of negative curvatures at that of its last finite-difference version. A Bofill update that opens another negative curvature has its sign flipped. When the ring model still shows more negative curvatures than the last exact blocks, finite differences rebuild the blocks, at most four times. On a 1203-coordinate aluminium slab the plain updates had grown 11 spurious negative curvatures.

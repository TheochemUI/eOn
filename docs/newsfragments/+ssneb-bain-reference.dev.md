test_solid_state_bain runs the solid-state band on the Bain path of switched LJ in a two-atom cell and checks the climbing image against a relaxed scan of c/a (barrier 0.0590356373 eV/atom at c/a 1.09331985). The NEB guide explains why the cell has two atoms: the saddle is unstable to the k = (1/2, 0, 1/2) shuffle, and a 5 x 5 x 4 supercell band leaves the tetragonal path for a lower one.

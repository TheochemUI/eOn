The L-BFGS and FIRE bands stepped through rgsaddle keep the mean force of an image when any atom is fixed, as the classic band does. Removing it pushed the fixed atoms, the host pinned them back after every step, and L-BFGS turned that correction into a negative-curvature reset each step: the EAM Al vacancy band and the Pt heptamer band cycled at max_move and stopped at max_iterations (7002 and 15907 force calls). They converge again in 240 and 79.

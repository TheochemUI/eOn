`[Potential] pair_switch_width` (Angstrom, default 0) turns on rgpot's C^2 quintic cutoff switch for `lj`, `ljcluster` and `morse_pt` and for those terms of `expr`, so the pair energy, force and curvature reach zero at the cutoff instead of the force jumping there. The key is in the Cap'n Proto parameter schema, the INI and JSON readers, `config.yaml` and `eon_schema.PotentialConfig`; an rgpot without the switch refuses a nonzero width.

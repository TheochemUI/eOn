`[AKMC] known_saddles` (default true) sends each min-mode process search the saddles already found from its state, both processes and saddles whose search failed after convergence (not connected, bad prefactor, barrier out of window, unconverged minima, failed prefactor, negative barrier). A climb point with negative curvature that matches one of them in energy and structure ends the search with status 23, "Climb reached a known saddle", before the endpoint minimizations. The server counts it as a repeat of that process, or as the same failed saddle. The list travels as the job invariant `known_saddles.dat`.

`[Xtsci] precon = rgmin_exp` hands the Packwood Exp preconditioner to rgmin under `opt_backend = rgmin` or `opt_method = xtsci`. eOn sends the minimum-image atom pairs within `2 r_nn`, and rgmin assembles the sparse matrix, estimates `mu` with one force call and solves it by conjugate gradients. The dense eOn `exp` kind is unchanged and stays the default path for `lbfgs_precon = exp`.

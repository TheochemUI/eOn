Saddle and process searches count the force calls made through the per-image potential copies that `Main.parallel` gives the dimer and Lanczos images of ext_pot, ASE, metatomic, VASP, LAMMPS and other single-threaded backends. `total_force_calls` and `force_calls_saddle` reported only the first image: an ext_pot dimer search of an LJ38 start made 300 calls and reported 51. Batched rgpot and host-surface calls are charged to the potential's own registry rather than the process default.

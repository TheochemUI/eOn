Process searches count every prefactor force call in `force_calls_prefactors` and `total_force_calls`. With `Main.parallel` the product Hessian runs on the second minimization's own potential instance and was left out: an ext_pot LJ13 search made 455 calls and reported 428. A search that ends at a failed prefactor Hessian now counts those calls too; one made 491 calls and reported 374 with `parallel` on or off.

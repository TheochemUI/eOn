`job = instanton` with `mode = rate` computes the thermal rate through a saddle below the crossover temperature, from the closed ring-polymer instanton of Richardson and Althorpe (doi:10.1063/1.3267318). It reads the reactant and `saddle.con`, takes `temperature` in kelvin, and writes `rate_instanton` to `results.dat`. At or above the crossover it stops without a rate. A cluster written inside a periodic cell keeps its rotations when those directions are zero modes of the reactant Hessian. The cubic-well check is the decay of Caldeira and Leggett (doi:10.1016/0003-4916(83)90202-6).

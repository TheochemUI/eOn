`job = instanton` with `mode = rate` computes the thermal rate through a saddle below the crossover temperature, from the closed ring-polymer instanton. It reads the reactant and `saddle.con`, takes `temperature` in kelvin, and writes `rate_instanton` to `results.dat`. At or above the crossover it stops without a rate. A cluster written inside a periodic cell keeps its rotations when those directions are zero modes of the reactant Hessian.

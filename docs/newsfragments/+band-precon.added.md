`[Nudged Elastic Band]` gains `band_precon`, `band_precon_mu` and `band_stepper` for the rgsaddle band. They are opt-in and experimental, and off by default. `band_precon = exp` selects rgsaddle's exponential band metric and `band_stepper = ode12r` its ODE12r stepper. Neither is the working method of Makri, Ortner and Kermode (2019) yet. That needs a fixed-atom mask for the metric and the preconditioned spring in rgsaddle. Until rgsaddle offers the mask, `exp` is refused when any atom is fixed. On `examples/neb-al`, `exp` raised the force calls of every converged case, and ODE12r stalled at a band force between 6e-3 and 1.5e-2 eV/Å, short of the default 1e-3. Both keys need eOn compiled against rgsaddle 0.3.0 or newer and a loaded library of C ABI minor 22 or newer. Otherwise eOn refuses them before it creates the band. The metric is also refused with `solid_state`, one atom per image, `energy_weighted` and `onsager_machlup`.

With rgsaddle linked, a solid_state band with opt_method lbfgs, fire or xtsci runs on rgsaddle's solid-state band (rgsaddle ABI minor 17): the cells move with the atoms and the convergence force is rebuilt every step. It used to step the atoms with every cell fixed and report the first step's force until max_iterations. An rgsaddle older than minor 17 refuses the combination with an error.

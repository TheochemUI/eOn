A solid-state band refuses a cell whose perpendicular width is not strictly greater than twice the cutoff of a periodic minimum-image pair potential (rgpot Lennard-Jones and Morse). At twice the cutoff a minimum-image tie is an energy cusp: the force jumps and is not the energy gradient, so FIRE halved its time step on every step and stopped with `[FIRE] m_dt is too small`, and L-BFGS ran to `max_iterations`. The band now names the cutoff and the cell width and stops before the first step. Fixed-cell bands and the optimizers are unchanged. Nonperiodic kernels, including ZBL, report no minimum-image cutoff and are not checked.

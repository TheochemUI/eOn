A solid-state band refuses a cell whose perpendicular width is under twice the cutoff of a minimum-image pair potential (rgpot Lennard-Jones, Morse, ZBL). In such a cell the kernel's force is not the gradient of its energy, so FIRE halved its time step on every step and stopped with `[FIRE] m_dt is too small`, and L-BFGS ran to `max_iterations`. The band now names the cutoff and the cell width and stops before the first step. Fixed-cell bands and the optimizers are unchanged.

`lbfgs_accept = energy` (and `nonmonotone`, and `lbfgs_secant = zhangxu`) no longer tests the band or min-mode saddle objective's energy, which its gradient does not descend: the band energy leaves out the springs and the climbing image goes up it, and the saddle search climbs along the mode. The test rejected good steps and spent up to ten halvings each, so the Pt heptamer bands of the optbench `eon/neb-pt-lbfgs` input averaged 3513 force calls instead of 317. Those objectives now take the plain step and log a warning. The `lbfgs.accept` and `xtsci.accept` member defaults read `energy` although every `Parameters` sets `none`; they now say `none`.

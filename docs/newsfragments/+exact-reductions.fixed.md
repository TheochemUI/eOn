Optimizer decisions no longer depend on the Eigen release, the compiler, or the vector width. L-BFGS, CG, FIRE, Quickmin and steepest descent decide on correctly rounded dot products and norms (`eonc::exact`). The step caps and the norm convergence tests use them too. The mean force removed after an evaluation is an exact column sum, so that subtraction does not follow Eigen's packet width. The LJ pair loop keeps contraction off for the distance square and the 12-6 products and fuses only the explicit force update, including on ARM where the x86 FMA clone is not built. The basin-hopping LJ13 case takes 1640 force calls with Eigen 3.4 and 5.0 under gcc 14 and 15; it took 1640 and 1700 before.

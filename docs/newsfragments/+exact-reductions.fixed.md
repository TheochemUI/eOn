Optimizer decisions no longer depend on the Eigen release, the compiler, or the vector width. L-BFGS, CG, FIRE, Quickmin and steepest descent decide on correctly rounded dot products and norms (`eonc::exact`). The step caps and the norm convergence tests use them too. The basin-hopping LJ13 case takes 1640 force calls with Eigen 3.4 and 5.0 under gcc 14 and 15; it took 1640 and 1700 before.
